Ceremony Flow & Run of Show

Complete step-by-step ceremony order from arrival to exit with all duties and hand-offs as they occur.

1

Stationing & Final Readiness

3:50 PM

Groomsmen finish ushering guests and meet the rest of the party at the back of the venue. Everyone lines up in pairs and stands by for music cue. Officiant Tara is stationed at the front.

2

Music Begins & Family Seating

4:00 PM

When processional music starts, Griffin escorts Melissa down the aisle to her front-row seat. He then walks back up the aisle to escort Jacqueline down to her seat. Griffin moves to the altar to stand with Tara.

3

Wedding Party Processional

Walking Guidelines:
  • Bridesmaids on the Left, Groomsmen on the Right.
  • Walk at a natural, comfortable pace. Bridesmaids hold bouquets in front; groomsmen keep hands folded.
  • Next pair starts walking when the pair ahead reaches 3/4 of the way down the aisle.
  • At the Altar, split to your side and slide all the way to the far outer ends so subsequent pairs have of room.
  1. 1. Alice & Reid
  2. 2. Alison & Keenan
  3. 3. Daniel & Calum
  4. 4. Emily & Matthew
4

Flower Girls & Ring Bearer

Once all wedding party pairs are in place at the altar, Ava pulls Cassian down the aisle in the wagon, and Madilyn follows right behind scattering flower petals.

  • Jacqueline: Steps out to lift Cassian out of the wagon to sit with her for the ceremony.
  • Daniel: Collects the wedding rings from Cassian at the wagon and keeps them secure.
5

Bride's Entrance & Bouquet Hand-Off

Corinne walks down the aisle. Once Corinne reaches Griffin at the altar, she hands her bouquet to Emily. Emily holds both bouquets for the ceremony duration.

6

Ring Exchange (Daniel)

During the exchange of vows and rings, Daniel passes the rings forward to Tara as requested.

7

Registry Signing (Emily & Matthew)

Tara directs Corinne, Griffin, Emily, and Matthew to the signing table. Emily rests Corinne’s bouquet in front of the registry book as a photo prop. After Corinne and Griffin sign, Emily and Matthew sign as the legal witnesses. Emily picks Corinne's bouquet back up as everyone returns to their front positions.

8

Announcement & Bouquet Return

Tara introduces Corinne & Griffin as married for the very first time! Before Corinne steps down the aisle, Emily hands the bridal bouquet back to Corinne.

9

Recessional Exit

Exit Gap Required

Corinne and Griffin walk down the aisle together first. Wedding party wait to begin walking until Corinne & Griffin have reached the very end of the aisle and begin exiting the venue.

  1. 1. Corinne & Griffin
  2. 2. Emily & Matthew
  3. 3. Daniel & Calum
  4. 4. Alison & Keenan
  5. 5. Alice & Reid

Everyone proceeds outside for wedding party and family portraits.
