Ceremony Flow & Mechanics

Our officiant, Tara, will be stationed at the front and will cue the processional music and entrances.

Processional Order (Entrance)

  1. Griffin escorts Melissa to her seat
  2. Griffin returns to escort Jacqueline to her seat
  3. Alice & Reid
  4. Alison & Keenan
  5. Daniel & Calum
  6. Emily & Matthew
  7. Ava & Madilyn (pulling Cassian in the wagon)
  8. Corinne escorted by Jeff
Note: Once at the altar, Jacqueline lifts Cassian out of the wagon to sit with her during the vows.

Recessional Order (Exit)

Cue from Tara:
"Family and friends please stand, it is my great honour and privilege to present to you for the very first time... Mr. Thomas and Mrs. Thomas!"

  1. Corinne & Griffin
  2. Emily & Matthew
  3. Daniel & Calum
  4. Alison & Keenan
  5. Alice & Reid

Registry Signing Mechanics (Emily & Matthew)

  1. Following the kiss, Tara directs Corinne, Griffin, Emily, and Matthew to the signing table.
  2. Emily brings Corinne’s bouquet and rests it on the table near the registry as a photo prop.
  3. Corinne and Griffin sign where indicated by Tara.
  4. Emily and Matthew sign next as the official legal witnesses.
  5. Emily takes the bouquet, everyone returns to altar positions, and Emily returns the bouquet to Corinne right before the recessional cue.
